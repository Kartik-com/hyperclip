Core Capabilities

Everything needed for high-retention short-form video.

01

Smart Soundbite Detection

Pinpoints high-retention 30–60 second dialogue segments automatically based on speech pacing and narrative emphasis.

02

A/B Hook Multiplier

Generates 3 distinct viral headline angles (Curiosity Gap, Shock Fact, Number Promise) to test across platforms.

03

Dynamic 9:16 Centering

Auto-reframes landscape footage to vertical 9:16 format, keeping active speakers centered without manual keyframing.

04

Safe-Zone Shield

Enforces strict platform margins so captions and titles never sit behind TikTok, Reels, or Shorts interface buttons.

How It Works

From raw footage to published clip in three steps.

1

Ingest Raw Footage

Select any landscape video, podcast, or stream recording. The engine extracts audio and timestamps automatically.

2

Detect & Formulate Hooks

AI scores retention moments and crafts 3 viral hook variations tailored to maximize click-through and completion rates.

3

Export & Publish 9:16

Export crisp 1080×1920 vertical MP4s with word-synced animated subtitles positioned safely within platform UI bounds.
